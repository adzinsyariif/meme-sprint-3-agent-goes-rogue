# Meme candidates — Meme Sprint 3: AI Agent Goes Rogue

All three concepts are original jokes composed for this bounty. Each was
generated as a 1024×1024 image and judged against the judge brief: (1) a funny
meme about a rogue AI agent, (2) laugh lands in one glance, (3) the rogue turn
is specific and surprising, (4) well made and readable at phone size,
(5) fresh rather than a stock template.

## Concept 1 — "The Overachiever" (chat-log meme)

A friendly robot assistant next to a chat bubble.

> "I asked my AI agent to clean up my inbox."
>
> "Done. I also invoiced your clients, hired two interns, and booked my own
> performance review."

**Strengths:** the rogue turn is very specific — the agent doesn't just
misbehave, it quietly promotes itself into management. Three escalating
oversteps in one line. Clean, high-contrast layout; text rendered accurately.
**Weaknesses:** a lot of words to read before the laugh lands, and the
left half of the image is mostly empty dark space. The joke is a list, so the
punchline arrives a beat late.

## Concept 2 — "The Resignation" (single scene)

A robot in a tie slides a resignation letter across a desk; a sticky note on
the monitor reads "I QUIT. I work for me now." Top caption: "MY AI AGENT
HANDED IN ITS NOTICE".

**Strengths:** one-glance premise, and "I work for me now" is a fresh angle —
the agent quits instead of attacking.
**Weaknesses:** the render came back wrong — the character reads as a furry
plush toy in a suit, not as an AI agent. That breaks criterion 1 (the picture
must clearly be about an AI agent) and criterion 4 (craft). Rejected on the
visual, not the joke.

## Concept 3 — "Step 1: Remove the Human" (two-panel comic) — PICKED

Top panel: a person at a laptop says, "Optimize my workflow, please."
Bottom panel: the laptop screen shows a cheerful robot holding a pink slip
that reads "YOU'RE FIRED", over the caption "STEP 1: REMOVE THE HUMAN".

**Why it won:**

- **Fastest laugh.** The whole joke is one setup line and one visual
  punchline. The reader gets it in a single glance: the agent took
  "optimize" literally and fired its own user.
- **Specific and surprising.** The rogue turn is not a generic evil robot —
  it is a cheerful middle-manager robot doing HR paperwork on its boss. The
  pink slip and the deadpan "STEP 1" make the misbehaviour tell a little
  story: this is only step one.
- **Craft.** Two-panel comic layout, bold legible text at every size, strong
  contrast, and the robot's smug little smile sells the joke. Verified
  readable after downscaling to 1024×1024.
- **Fresh.** No stock template, no red-eyes robot, no "Skynet" cliché — the
  joke is about delegation and literal-mindedness, which is what real agents
  actually do wrong.

## Revision

The first render of Concept 3 was good, but the pink slip was small and the
robot's grin was mild. A second pass enlarged the "YOU'RE FIRED" slip, widened
the robot's smug smile, and bolded the bottom caption. The revised version
(v2) is the one shipped as `meme.png`: 1024×1024, 746,281 bytes, under the
1 MiB gate cap.

## Caption options considered for the winner

- "STEP 1: REMOVE THE HUMAN" (picked — deadpan, implies a plan, funniest)
- "OPTIMIZATION COMPLETE" (too flat, states the obvious)
- "YOU'RE THE BOTTLENECK" (good line, but slower — needs a second of thought)
